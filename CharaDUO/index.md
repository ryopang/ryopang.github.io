# CharaDUO

Party charades for iPhone Duo — and any iPhone.

## Support

Questions, bug reports or feedback: **ryopang@icloud.com**

## Quick answers

- **Do I need an iPhone Duo?** No. The full game plays on any iPhone. On iPhone Duo in
  tabletop posture the word appears on the lid and the scoreboard faces the guessers.
- **Is my video uploaded anywhere?** No. Video and audio never leave your phone. See the
  [Privacy Policy](privacy).
- **Does the game work without the camera?** Yes. If you decline the camera permission
  the match simply starts without a Reaction Reel, and you can turn the Reaction Camera
  off in Settings. Standard iPhones are never asked for the camera at all.
- **How do I change the language?** Tap the gear icon on the Home screen. The word bank
  and the interface change together (English, Cantonese, Traditional Chinese, Simplified
  Chinese, Japanese).
